English: Coverage zone planning for airports / DJI Dock. Generates 5 km geodesic coverage circles (theoretical limit; 4–5 km recommended for initial siting), draggable and recalculable, with live statistics on overlap and blind zones. Six online basemaps (Esri, Google, GEOVIS, Siwei, Jilin-1, Tianditu with user-supplied key), built-in China province/city/county boundaries loaded on demand, line & area measurement, GeoPackage export, and one-click journal-style figure export (PDF/PNG/SVG/TIFF with locator inset, scale bar, legend, zonal statistics and graticule).

Requirements / restrictions: QGIS >= 3.22, pure Python + Qt (PyQGIS), no third-party Python dependencies. Online basemaps and administrative boundaries require internet access; Tianditu requires the user to supply their own API key in the plugin panel.
